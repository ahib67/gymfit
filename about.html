Our Story

From a garage gym to Lahore's training ground

PulseFit started in 2014 as a small garage setup with a squat rack and a handful of regulars. Over a decade later, we've grown into a full-scale training facility — but the mindset hasn't changed: no shortcuts, no fads, just consistent, well-coached training that actually works.

  • ✓
    Coached, not just supervised — every plan is built around you.
  • ✓
    Community first — members train together and push each other.
  • ✓
    Evidence-based programming — no gimmicks, just what works.
